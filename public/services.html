Our Services

Group 01

Assessment & Diagnostics

No treatment without diagnosis. Every programme at Quantum begins the same way: with a complete, physician-led picture of your health.

Prevention is the foundation of everything we do. Before any therapy is considered, our physicians evaluate your medical history, your results and your risk profile — and only then build a plan. Most diagnostic answers return within hours from our on-campus laboratory, not days.

Executive and corporate wellness programmes are available for leaders who need the same rigour around their schedules. Explore executive assessment →

01

Comprehensive Medical Examination

Full physical evaluation, medical and family history review, lifestyle and risk-factor assessment, early detection of concerns, and a clear set of recommendations.

02

Preventive Health Assessment

Early disease-risk identification, structured screening, personalised preventive strategies and regular health monitoring — prevention measured in outcomes, not checklists.

03

Precision Diagnostics

Blood investigations, body composition, skin and hair analysis, biomarker assessment, cardiac evaluation, ultrasound imaging and health-risk analysis — one integrated diagnostic core, answers in hours.

04

Hormonal Assessment

Thyroid, adrenal and reproductive hormone profiles, cortisol and metabolic balance — the chemistry that drives energy, mood and ageing, measured and rebalanced.

05

Genetic Testing

DNA risk analysis, epigenetic ageing, nutrigenomics and pharmacogenomics — inherited risk, understood early, acted on before it becomes disease.

06

Functional Medicine Assessment

Root-cause analysis: gut microbiome, inflammation markers, nutritional status and lifestyle factors — treating causes rather than managing symptoms.

07

Cardiovascular & Metabolic Screening

ECG, blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose, insulin resistance and lipid profiles — the earliest signals of the world's most common diseases.

08

Personalised Health Planning

Your roadmap: treatment protocols, nutrition and exercise planning, lifestyle modification and ongoing progress monitoring — a plan that stays current as you change.

Group 02

Regenerative Medicine

Where conventional medicine manages decline, regenerative medicine aims to restore function at the level where it begins — the cell.

Cellular therapies, immunotherapies and growth-factor treatments are matched to your diagnostics and offered only where medically appropriate and permitted by regulation. Every regenerative pathway at Quantum is physician-led, evidence-informed and documented — from source and processing to administration and follow-up.

Deep dive: how our cell therapies are selected, processed and delivered. Visit Regenerative Medicine →

01

Mesenchymal Stem Cell (MSC) Therapy

MSCs support tissue repair and modulate inflammation — the workhorse of our regenerative programmes, used across a range of conditions.

02

Allogeneic MSC Therapy

Healthy donor-derived cells, processed in regulated facilities, offering consistent, reproducible regenerative material.

03

Organ-Specific Progenitor Cells

Targeted regenerative support for specific organs, from liver to heart to pancreas — selected by diagnosis, not by habit.

04

Adipose-Derived Stem Cells

Cells harvested from your own adipose tissue — an abundant, minimally invasive source for personalised regenerative therapy.

05

Bone Marrow Stem Cells

One of the most studied sources of regenerative cells, supporting healing response and tissue function.

06

Wharton's Jelly Stem Cells

Young, highly proliferative cells from umbilical cord tissue — valued for their regenerative potential and low immunogenicity.

07

Amniotic Stem Cells & Placenta Extracts

Rich in growth factors, supporting tissue healing, skin vitality and immune balance.

08

Exosome Therapy

Cell-free regenerative signals: exosomes carry the communication molecules of repair without the cells themselves — a growing area of regenerative medicine.

09

Immunotherapy

NK-T and CAR-T programmes designed to strengthen immune defence — personalised cellular immunotherapy under expert clinical monitoring.

10

Growth Factor Therapy

Concentrated growth factors and PRP to accelerate natural healing in joints, tendons and soft tissue.

11

Cellular Repair & Tissue Regeneration

Programmes supporting recovery of damaged cells and tissues — from post-injury repair to immune regeneration.

12

Cellular Regeneration Programmes

Comprehensive, physician-monitored pathways combining cell therapies, IV support and follow-up — regeneration as a process, not a procedure.

Group 03

Longevity & Healthy Ageing

Age is counted in years. Health is counted in cells. Longevity medicine works on the second number.

Healthy ageing at Quantum is not a promise of immortality — it is a discipline of measurement. We assess biological age, cellular stress, hormonal balance and organ function, then design interventions that target the ageing process itself: nutrition, exercise, sleep, hormonal support and, where appropriate, regenerative therapies.

01

Biological & Epigenetic Age Assessment

Measures how your body is ageing compared with your calendar age — the clearest single metric for longevity planning.

02

Longevity Programmes

Personalised multi-month pathways: preventive therapies, cellular health optimisation, wellness coaching and quarterly re-measurement of progress.

03

Anti-Ageing Evaluation

Biological age, skin and cellular health, oxidative stress and hormonal status — the full audit of how you are ageing.

04

Hormone Balance & Optimisation

Restoring the endocrine signals that govern energy, strength, cognition and recovery — measured, then rebalanced under medical supervision.

05

Healthy Ageing Monitoring

Repeat biomarker assessments and physician review on a schedule designed for your biology — progress you can see in the numbers.

Group 04

Integrative & Complementary Care

Adjuvant therapies that support recovery — intravenous nutrition, detoxification and regenerative support, always under medical supervision.

These therapies are not alternatives to medicine; they are its reinforcements. Each one is administered by clinical staff, matched to your assessment, and used to support cellular repair, immune function and recovery — whether you are preparing for a regenerative programme or simply rebuilding resilience.

01

Peptide Therapy

Bioactive peptides supporting cellular repair, recovery, metabolism and hormonal balance.

02

IV Nutrient Therapy

Vitamins, minerals and hydration delivered directly into the bloodstream — absorption without the digestive bottleneck.

03

High-Dose Vitamin C Therapy

Antioxidant and immune support at clinical doses, commonly used alongside recovery protocols.

04

Meyer's Cocktail & Multivitamin Infusions

Classic formulations for energy, immune function and general repletion.

05

Ozone & Hydrogen Therapy

Blood oxygenation and selective antioxidant support — two well-established modalities in regenerative and sports medicine.

06

Chelation & Plasmapheresis

Medical removal of heavy metals and harmful plasma components — detoxification performed as clinical procedures, not fads.

07

Detoxification Programmes

Structured biological detox pathways supporting liver, kidney and cellular function, with clear start and end criteria.

08

Microbiome Restoration & Gut Healing

Rebuilding intestinal flora and repair of the gut lining — the foundation of immunity, mood and absorption.

09

Wellness Retreats

Residential programmes combining assessment, nutrition, fitness and recovery on campus. Explore retreats →

Group 05

Physical Therapy & Rehabilitation

Recovery is not an event; it is a programme. We restore strength, mobility and function with a structured, measurable plan.

From post-surgical recovery to stroke rehabilitation and sports performance, every rehab pathway at Quantum is built around your functional baseline, reviewed at intervals and adjusted as you improve — with physiotherapists and physicians working from the same plan.

01

Musculoskeletal Rehabilitation

Joint, muscle and spine recovery — reducing pain and stiffness, restoring range, strength and function.

02

Neurological Rehabilitation

Coordination, balance and cognitive recovery for conditions affecting the nervous system.

03

Stroke Rehabilitation

Strength, speech, mobility and daily-living skills — maximising recovery in the critical months after a stroke.

04

Sports Rehabilitation

Injury recovery, strength restoration and reconditioning for a safe return to play — and prevention of the next injury.

05

Pain Management

Acute and chronic pain treated with physical therapy, targeted interventions and, where appropriate, regenerative options — with the goal of reducing dependence on medication.

06

Mobility Restoration

Gait, balance and flexibility work to restore independent movement at every age.

07

Functional Rehabilitation

Training for real life: endurance, coordination and independence in the activities that matter to you.

08

Post-Treatment Recovery Programmes

Structured recovery after medical treatment — restoring performance and preventing complications.

Alternative Medicine

Nature has protocols too. We practice them with the same discipline as we practice medicine — assessed, matched and monitored.

Alternative medicine at Quantum is not a rejection of science; it is an extension of it. Holistic, functional and lifestyle approaches are used alongside conventional care, chosen for what the evidence and your assessment support — food and habits treated as medicine.

01

Holistic Medicine

Body, mind and environment treated as one system — balance as the therapeutic target.

02

Integrative Medicine

Conventional and natural therapies combined into a single, coherent plan.

03

Functional Medicine

Root-cause identification and correction — restoring function rather than suppressing symptoms.

04

Nutritional Medicine

Correction of deficiencies and personalised dietary therapy as a primary intervention.

05

Lifestyle Medicine

Sleep, movement, stress and weight as clinical variables — the highest-evidence interventions in medicine.

06

Detox & Natural Healing Programmes

Evidence-based natural therapies supporting the body's own repair systems.

07

Complementary Medical Therapies

Supportive therapies alongside conventional treatment — reducing discomfort and accelerating recovery.

Group 07

Aesthetics & Dermatology

Medical dermatology and regenerative aesthetics — skin, hair and soft tissue treated with clinical rigour, not vanity.

Our dermatology centre combines medical and cosmetic dermatology, laser and energy-based treatments, hair restoration and regenerative options such as PRP, exosome and MSC-based therapies — offered where appropriate as complements, never as routine anti-ageing promises.

01

Medical Dermatology

Acne, eczema, psoriasis, rosacea, vitiligo, skin infections, nail and hair disorders — diagnosed and treated with evidence-based dermatology.

02

Skin Rejuvenation

Texture, tone, collagen and hydration — programmes for ageing skin, sun damage and dullness.

03

Hair Restoration

Pattern hair loss in men and women, scalp health and PRP hair therapy — restoration, not masks.

04

Scar & Wound Care

Acne, surgical, burn and keloid scars — plus chronic wound care and skin regeneration.

05

Laser & Energy-Based Treatments

Pigmentation, resurfacing, vascular lesions, tightening and hair removal — selected by skin assessment, delivered by clinicians.

06

Regenerative Aesthetics

PRP, exosome and MSC-based protocols for skin and hair — regenerative support for selected indications.
