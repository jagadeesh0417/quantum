Division 01

Medical Check-Ups — Anti-Ageing & Regenerative Medicine

Comprehensive medical assessments built around prevention, early detection, and personalised planning for healthy ageing.

What It Is

Medical check-ups at Quantum are comprehensive health assessments built around prevention, early detection, and personalised planning for healthy ageing. Unlike a single annual blood panel, they combine physician consultation, advanced diagnostics, and anti-ageing evaluation into one integrated assessment.

The focus is early detection, disease prevention, performance optimisation, healthy ageing, and long-term well-being. Every assessment ends with a clear, actionable report and a personalised health plan — not a checklist of numbers.

How It Works

01

Doctor Consultation

Physician-led review of your medical history, family history and current concerns.

02

Diagnostics

Comprehensive investigations covering all key body systems — most answers return within hours from our on-campus laboratory.

03

Physician Review

Your results are read together with your risk profile and lifestyle — diagnosis first, always.

04

Personalised Plan

Treatment protocols, nutrition and exercise planning, lifestyle modification and ongoing progress monitoring.

Indications

Medical check-up programmes may be appropriate for individuals who:

  • Want to establish a complete baseline of their health
  • Have demanding leadership or executive roles
  • Have risk factors such as family history, stress, or sedentary work
  • Wish to prevent age-related diseases and support healthy ageing
  • Want early detection of silent conditions such as metabolic or cardiovascular risk
  • Desire personalised preventive healthcare and annual evaluations

Contraindications

Diagnostic procedures are generally safe. Certain investigations or therapies within a programme may not be appropriate for everyone — for example:

  • Pregnancy (certain imaging or functional investigations)
  • Known allergy to contrast or testing components
  • Severe claustrophobia (certain imaging studies)
  • Inability to undergo the required procedures
  • Any condition that the treating physician determines makes a test or therapy unsuitable

Each programme begins with a physician consultation so that every investigation is chosen for you — never applied as a routine.

Division 02

Cellular Therapies, Immunotherapies & Stem Cells

Advanced regenerative and immune-based therapies focused on cellular renewal, tissue repair, and long-term wellness.

What It Is

Cellular therapies use stem cells — the body's raw material — to support repair, replace, or regenerate damaged tissue, addressing the underlying cause of a condition rather than only its symptoms. Immunotherapies strengthen immune defence, and growth-factor treatments accelerate natural healing.

Every regenerative pathway at Quantum is physician-led, evidence-informed and documented — from source and processing to administration and follow-up. Cellular therapies are offered only where medically appropriate and permitted by regulation.

04

Treatment

Delivered by trained practitioners under medical supervision, with safety prioritised.

05

Support & Recovery

Aftercare and, where relevant, supporting therapies to consolidate results.

06

Follow-Up

Progress reviewed and the plan refined, with repeat testing where useful.

Indications

Regenerative protocols may be considered for a wide range of conditions — from age-related degeneration to organ-specific disorders. Cellular therapies may be considered for individuals with:

  • Age-related functional decline
  • Orthopaedic, joint or musculoskeletal conditions
  • Neurological and neurodegenerative conditions
  • Cardiovascular, metabolic or endocrine disorders
  • Autoimmune and immune-related conditions
  • Recovery goals after injury, surgery or treatment

Regenerative therapies may not be appropriate for everyone. Contraindications or situations requiring caution may include:

  • Active severe infection
  • Uncontrolled systemic illness

  • Severe bleeding disorders or uncontrolled coagulation disorders
  • Uncontrolled autoimmune disease (depending on the therapy)

  • Severe uncontrolled heart failure or unstable cardiovascular disease
