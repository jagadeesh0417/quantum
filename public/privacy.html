Privacy Policy

How Quantum Health Biotech Park collects, uses, and protects your personal and medical information.

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quantum Health Biotech Park ("we", "our", "the Park") collects, uses, stores, and protects the information you share with us through this website, our enquiry process, and your care journey. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal and medical information with the highest standards of confidentiality and security.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly and information collected automatically when you use this website:

  • Contact details — name, email address, telephone number, and country of residence.
  • Enquiry information — details you share in our consultation request forms, including health goals and preferred programme.
  • Medical information — information provided during consultation or through pre-arrival medical questionnaires, including medical history, current medications, and health records.
  • Technical information — browser type, device, pages visited, and referral source, collected through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Correspondence — records of communications with our team, including email and telephone conversations.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to enquiries and arrange consultations and visits.
  • To assess suitability and prepare personalised assessment and treatment plans.
  • To coordinate your care, accommodation, transfers, and follow-up.
  • To communicate programme details, appointments, and important updates.
  • To improve our website, services, and guest experience.
  • To send newsletters or updates you have requested (you may unsubscribe at any time).
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Medical Data & Confidentiality

Your medical information is treated as strictly confidential. It is accessed only by the physicians and care staff directly involved in your assessment and treatment, and is never sold, rented, or shared for marketing purposes. Medical records are handled in accordance with applicable healthcare privacy laws and professional obligations of confidentiality.

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not share your personal information with third parties except: with your consent; with service providers who support our operations (such as hospitality and logistics partners) under confidentiality obligations; where required by law or regulatory authorities; or to protect the rights, safety, and security of the Park, our guests, and the public.

5. International Transfers

As an international healthcare destination, we may receive medical records and correspondence from guests travelling from other countries. Your information may be processed in India, where the Park is located, under safeguards designed to protect your data to a standard consistent with this policy.

6. Data Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, or disclosure — including secure storage, access controls, and encrypted transmission of sensitive data where applicable.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your information where permitted by law.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

8. Cookies & Analytics

This website uses cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use the site and to improve performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the website.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal and medical information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, and support continuity of care and follow-up.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us at info@quantumhealthbiotech.com or by post at Quantum Health Biotech Park, Kanakapura Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.
