The Land

A private environment designed around care, recovery and research.

Lakes, orchards and gardens in the green lung of Bengaluru — 40 minutes from the international airport.

Fourteen destinations sit within the park — from reception and luxury villas to a private lake, research block and helipad. The resort environment is not a backdrop; it is part of the protocol.

Cottages, suites, fine dining and concierge — a five-star stay with medical care next door. Spa, yoga, hydrotherapy, nutrition and mindfulness are woven into every programme.

Medicine & Research

Clinical science, on campus.

Every programme begins with a complete, physician-led picture of your health.

An Advanced Diagnostics Centre delivers molecular, functional and imaging technologies with most answers returning within hours from our on-campus laboratory — not days. The Regenerative Medicine Institute houses purpose-built cellular therapy suites for evidence-informed regenerative medicine.

An Executive Health Centre offers comprehensive assessments, preventive screenings and personalised health optimisation programmes — while on-campus biotechnology and translational research with global partners, and a training academy for physicians and care teams, extend the campus beyond the clinic.

  • Advanced Diagnostics Centre
  • Regenerative Medicine Institute
  • Executive Health Centre
  • Biotechnology Research Facilities
  • International Training Academy
Wellness Destinations

Nature-led recovery, woven into every stay.

Wellness at Quantum is not an amenity — it is the protocol.

An infinity reflection pool, botanical healing gardens and a medicinal plant garden frame the park, while a yoga and meditation pavilion hosts guided practice, breathing work and holistic programmes. The hydrotherapy centre and lakeside luxury spa support relaxation, rehabilitation and recovery.

Forest walking trails encourage mindful movement, and the private lake offers a tranquil waterfront for reflection and gentle wellness activities — every space designed around a single question: what does a body in recovery need?

  • Infinity Reflection Pool
  • Yoga & Meditation Pavilion
  • Hydrotherapy Centre
  • Luxury Spa
  • Botanical & Medicinal Gardens
