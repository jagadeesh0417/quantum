Who We Are

Transforming the Future of Wellness

22 acres on Kanakapura Road One campus where medicine, biotechnology, wellness, research and hospitality converge.

Quantum Health Biotech Park represents a new generation of integrated healthcare destinations where advanced medicine, biotechnology, regenerative medicine, preventive healthcare, luxury wellness, research, education, and hospitality converge within a single world-class campus.

Situated on a beautifully designed 22-acre campus on Kanakapura Road, Bengaluru, our vision is to create an environment where individuals can prevent disease, optimise health, accelerate recovery, and embrace healthy ageing through personalised, evidence-informed healthcare.

Our multidisciplinary team brings together expertise in preventive medicine, regenerative medicine, lifestyle medicine, diagnostics, nutrition, physiotherapy, rehabilitation, aesthetic medicine, longevity science, and wellness coaching to provide comprehensive, patient-centred care.

Every programme begins with an in-depth understanding of each individual's health profile, lifestyle, genetics, risk factors, and long-term goals, allowing us to create highly personalised health optimisation programmes.

Our Story

Our Journey

Quantum Health Biotech Park was conceived with a vision to redefine healthcare by creating a destination where science, technology, wellness, and hospitality exist in perfect harmony.

Recognising the increasing global demand for preventive healthcare, healthy ageing, regenerative medicine, and personalised wellness, our founders envisioned a healthcare ecosystem that extends beyond traditional hospitals.

Our integrated model combines advanced diagnostics, physician-led care, biotechnology innovation, luxury wellness experiences, research, education, and sustainable infrastructure to support individuals throughout every stage of life.

Meet Our Founders

Leadership with Vision

Dr. Vikas Singh

Founder & Director

Dr. Vikas Singh brings more than two decades of experience across preventive medicine, longevity science and clinical leadership. Trained at leading institutions in Europe and Asia, he has spent his career at the intersection of evidence-based medicine and personalised care — designing programmes that treat the person behind the patient file.

As Founder & Director, he leads the clinical vision of the park: a root-cause, measurement-first approach where no protocol begins before the diagnostics are complete.

Achievements
